# Avera

Avera is an open-source personal finance and wealth management platform designed to help you understand, manage, and grow your finances.

Track multiple wallets and financial accounts—including cash, bank accounts, investments, gold, and other assets—in one place. Record income and expenses, monitor your financial position, and gain insights through monthly and annual reports.

Avera is built with simplicity, transparency, and extensibility in mind, making it easy to self-host, customize, and contribute to.

## Features

* 💰 Multiple wallets and financial accounts
* 💵 Income and expense tracking
* 🏦 Bank account management
* 📈 Investment tracking
* 🪙 Gold and other asset tracking
* 📊 Monthly and annual financial reports
* 💎 Net worth tracking
* 📅 Financial history and summaries
* 🔒 Self-hostable and privacy-focused
* 🧩 Extensible and community-driven

## Open Source

Avera is free and open source. You can use, modify, self-host, and contribute to the project according to its license.

Whether you want to track your personal finances, build your own financial dashboard, or contribute new features, Avera is designed to be a foundation for better personal financial management.
